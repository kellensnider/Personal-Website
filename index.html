Thesis prospectus

The problem

Aircraft that don't broadcast ADS-B can be missing from a cockpit traffic display, so pilots have to spot them by eye. Atlas is on-device computer vision that finds them from an iPhone camera in the cockpit, targeting detection at 1 to 2 miles.

What I'm building

  • A detector for the iPhone camera, trained on Amazon's Airborne Object Tracking dataset plus our own flight footage.
  • A cockpit data-collection pipeline that records video and telemetry next to a team-built Stratux ADS-B receiver, then uses the ADS-B data to label the footage for training.
  • The test program: I flew 2 instrumented flight tests as pilot in command. Flight 1 logged 82 minutes of video (148K frames) and 6,737 ADS-B reports.

Flight 2 result

On footage it had never seen, the detector (trained only on public data) locked onto an aircraft as the crew spotted it and held the track for the whole encounter, filtering about 300 candidates per frame.
