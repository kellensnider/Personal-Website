About

AI research built from the pilot's seat.

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of Oregon's Clark Honors College (GPA 3.97), pursuing minors in Mathematics, Political Science, and Entrepreneurship. I co-founded ATLAS AI as my honors thesis — a computer vision and machine learning startup building real-time traffic detection and situational awareness tools for single-pilot general aviation. That work earned a $5,000 Oregon Innovation Challenge Fellowship.

Alongside ATLAS AI, I engineer blockchain solutions at Oregon Blockchain Group, where I led development of TrustLedger — a trustless escrow and decentralized arbitration protocol on Arbitrum. I also do freelance web development as the technical lead of Sower Creative, building production sites for local businesses end-to-end.

I'm a licensed private pilot with 74.8 hours of flight time. That firsthand cockpit experience drives everything I build in aviation — I'm not solving a hypothetical problem.
