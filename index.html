01Featured

Atlas AI

Co-Founder, Lead Engineer & Test Pilot · Jan 2025 – present · Clark Honors College thesis

An on-device computer vision system that detects aircraft that don't broadcast ADS-B, using an iPhone camera in the cockpit. I lead engineering, built the data-collection pipeline, and flew the test flights as pilot in command.

Problem

Aircraft that don't broadcast ADS-B can be missing from a cockpit traffic display, so pilots have to spot them by eye.

What I built

  • An on-device detector for the iPhone camera, targeting detection at 1–2 miles. It is trained on Amazon's Airborne Object Tracking dataset plus our own flight footage.
  • A cockpit data-collection pipeline that records video and telemetry next to a team-built Stratux ADS-B receiver, then uses the ADS-B data to label the footage for training.

How I tested it

I flew 2 instrumented flight tests as pilot in command, then ran the detector on footage it had never seen.

Results

Atlas AI flight-test results
FlightResult
Flight 1 82 min of video (148K frames) and 6,737 ADS-B reports logged.
Flight 2 On unseen footage, the detector (trained only on public data) locked onto an aircraft as the crew spotted it and tracked it through the full 17 s encounter, filtering about 300 candidates per frame.
